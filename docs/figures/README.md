# Figures for the report

This directory is the include root the report actually reads. `docs/report/main.tex` is built from inside
`docs/report/`, and every graphic in the chapters is pulled in as
`\includegraphics[width=0.XX\textwidth]{../figures/NAME.png}`, i.e. from **`docs/figures/`**, not from
`docs/report/figures/`. The `.png` is what LaTeX includes; the `.pdf` of the same name is kept beside it for
print and for zooming into a single panel, and the two are written from the same matplotlib figure.

The `cavity_*`, `energy_drift`, `abc_duct`, `scatter-*`, `rcs-*` and `ms-*` files are study outputs, described in
`docs/OUTLINE.md` and regenerated by their studies; they are not documented again here.

## The mode-shape pair (C2b)

One figure, drawn twice, from two banked eigensolves of the same microstrip cross-section: PMC / open top
(`c1_spectrum.npz`, QZ column 738) and PEC / closed box (`pecbox_spectrum.npz`, QZ column 699). Each figure is a
single page laid out as columns PMC | PEC | text and rows analytic `e_z`, extracted `e_z`, difference, then the
same three for `e_y`. Panels, axes and colour limits are identical between the two figures — the limits are the
per-panel maximum over *both* metrics — so the before and after pictures can be flipped between without any panel
moving or rescaling.

The pair is a correction, and the correction is in one row only. **Before**: the `e_z` row correlated the analytic
*vertical* reference with an extracted *longitudinal* field (`e_z_superseded = -interp(beta*z_free)`), two different
physical components. The arithmetic is right and the comparison is wrong, which is why that row reads as a
catastrophic disagreement (correlation `0.1192` / `0.1473`, difference rows full-scale) when the mode is in fact
fine. **After**: the same row correlates vertical against vertical (`e_z_vv`, component 1 of `e_t` sampled on the
`A_ez` lattice), and it reads `0.9353` / `0.9369` with the difference rows down to `max|Δ|` `6.74` / `6.72`. The
`e_y` rows (width against width) were never affected and are identical in the two figures, `0.9208` / `0.9123`;
that row is the built-in control which shows the panels did not change between the two runs.

Nothing is solved to draw these. The scripts read the banked spectra and the banked eigenvectors and refuse to
draw unless all three banked correlations reproduce to `1e-6`.

## Provenance

* Generator: `tools/host/mode_plot/mode_plot.py` (driver `tools/host/mode_plot/run.sh`, log
  `tools/host/mode_plot/out/run.log`). It reconstructs `e_y` and `e_z_vv` from the banked `e_t` eigenvector with
  `emfit`'s `Mode2D.e_at` on the two plane lattices, and rebuilds the longitudinal `e_z` through the audit's own LU
  route — no new solve.
* Banked inputs: `tools/host/c1-audit/out/c1_spectrum.npz` (PMC) and `tools/host/c1-audit/out/pecbox_spectrum.npz`
  (PEC), produced by `c1_audit.py` / `pecbox_variant.py`.
* Numbers quoted above match the printed panel text on the figures (`corr_ez_vv` `+0.935287` / `+0.936930`,
  `corr_ez_superseded` `+0.119160` / `+0.147291`, `corr_ey` `+0.920771` / `+0.912343`, `eps_eff` `3.629512` /
  `3.606095`).

## Two caveats a reader of the report will hit

1. **The in-figure text is stale about its own filenames.** The text column prints the paths the script was
   hard-wired to at the time (`docs/report/figures/c2b_mode_shape_corrected.pdf` and
   `docs/design/mispaired_metric_before.pdf`). The files here are byte-identical copies of those, under the names
   the chapter uses; nothing inside the panels has been redrawn. Changing the printed path means editing
   `mode_plot.py` and re-running it.
2. **The page is already full size, so it cannot be read at `\textwidth`.** Each figure is one page of
   `921.3 × 793.8` pt (`12.80 × 11.02` in, landscape) with the panels rasterised at `200` dpi and the in-figure
   text drawn at roughly `7` pt. Set to `\textwidth` (~`6.5` in) that text prints at about `3.5` pt — legible only
   by zooming into the PDF. The chapter includes it at `0.95\textwidth` as a placeholder; the honest options are a
   rotated full-page float, a split into two half-figures (`e_z` row and `e_y` row), or a redraw with fewer panels
   and larger type.

## Duplicates kept on purpose

`docs/design/c2b_shapes.pdf` / `.png` (historical path, now holding the *corrected* figure — it is the corrected
one, not the mis-paired one, whatever the old name suggests), `docs/design/mispaired_metric_before.pdf` / `.png`
(the *before* figure) and `docs/report/figures/c2b_mode_shape_corrected.pdf` / `.png` (the generator's raw output
directory) are copies of the four files above. The PDFs and PNGs here match their sources byte for byte; verify with
`md5sum docs/figures/mode_shape_*.{pdf,png}`.

**Superseded, retained for provenance.** The `mode_shape_mispaired_before.*` and `mode_shape_corrected.*` pages
above are the earlier single-page form of the mode-shape pair, drawn from the same banked solves.
`docs/report/sec_modes.tex` now includes `fig_ez_before_after.pdf` and `fig_ey_before_after.pdf` instead, under the
labels `fig:mode-ez-pairing` and `fig:mode-ey-pairing`; the labels `fig:mode_shape_mispaired` and
`fig:mode_shape_corrected` no longer exist in that chapter. Do not cite either `mode_shape_*` file as current.
Nothing is deleted.
