B. Verdict
It does not move onto the critical path merely because absorbing termination removes the floor. These are different budgets. The floor is an accuracy number with zero wall cost; the eigensolve is a per-geometry, per-k0 cost that is not in the time-stepping loop and - on the production path - is not invoked at all. Removing an accuracy floor cannot promote a cost item; the two quantities do not trade.
It does become critical under one condition: the acceptance criteria require the full-wave mixed pencil as the run basis at a cross-section resolution where n_free_edges exceeds the structural cap. The cap is _MIXED_DENSE_MAX = 3000 at emfit/mode2d.py:185; above it solve_modes_mixed raises NotImplementedError ('iterative path pending: A - sigma*B is dense through C D C^T; dense QZ only'), with the reason documented alongside. Today the mixed pencil has been solved on the microstrip only at h=0.20 (1804 dof) and never above it (decisions.md:6768).
Flip quantity: n_free_edges of the S-parameter extraction cross-section, versus 3000. Two flip values matter and they are different numbers. The hard flip is n_free_edges ~ 3000 (emfit/mode2d.py:185): at or above it the mixed basis is simply unavailable. The practical flip is much lower: the per-k0 cost already equals 0.43 of the entire h=0.4 field solve (~100 s at 1804 vs 232.01 s), and a three-point k0 sweep at 1592 dof costs roughly as much as one full h=0.4 run.
One caveat on the verdict's premise: the tree disagrees with itself about which pencil is production. tools/d585_modal.py names solve_modes - ARPACK shift-invert, no cap - as the production engine and keeps the mixed QZ 'only for validation ... it is O(n^3) with a dense B block and dies on the boxes the sweep needs'; tools/host/sparse-probe/sparse_solver_test.py calls solve_modes_mixed 'The production route' and declares it 'dead above free_e ~ 2400'. If the former is right, the iterative roadmap has already landed on the pencil that matters and SLEPc buys nothing. If the latter is right, the roadmap is the only route past 2400. My reading of the production chain favours neither - production uses neither solver - but the flip-value argument above stands only under the second reading.

D. What the termination work changes
Zero wall-time saving and zero cells. It changes only which quantity is gated: AC-3 |S11| <= 1.0e-3 against a floor of 3.618e-4. The consequence is that a modal-basis error previously hidden under the floor becomes visible when the floor goes away - and that error is asserted at 3.06 % / 2.99 %, span 6.24 %, six times T1_TOL, which is why basis='mixed' is not gated and raises NotImplementedError in tools/gates_101_20.py:481-493. So termination pushes the accuracy bar down onto the mode/basis. That is the honest strongest case for promotion: it is an accuracy-driven, not cost-driven, promotion of the modal roadmap - and it is the only form of the claim that survives section A.
